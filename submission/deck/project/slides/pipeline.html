WHAT ASTRA DOES

From any sky position to graded evidence, live

Every step runs on real SPHEREx DR1 and QR3 images from NASA/IPAC IRSA. Nothing is precomputed except offline snapshots, which are labelled.

01

Resolve

Name or coordinates, with proper motion

02

Fetch

Every SPHEREx exposure of that spot

03

Stack

~6-month visits, blinked and differenced

04

Measure

Flux, position, 0.75–5 µm spectrum, motion

05

Check

Flags, blends, Gaia, SIMBAD, CatWISE, SkyBoT

06

Explain

AI restates evidence; cannot add numbers

07

Review

People record verdicts and export reports

FastAPI · Astropy · photutils · reproject on the backend. Next.js · Plotly · Aladin Lite on the frontend. Every view state lives in a shareable URL.

ASTRA · Space Apps 202603
The pipeline is the same for any coordinate on the sky. ASTRA queries IRSA’s SIA2 service for every SPHEREx exposure, streams small cutouts, subtracts the zodiacal light model, and stacks exposures into visits roughly six months apart. It predicts where the target should be at every exposure from its catalogued proper motion and parallax, measures it, and then checks the result against Gaia, SIMBAD, CatWISE and known asteroids before grading the evidence.
