ASTRA stands for Astronomical Search and Temporal Research Assistant. It's a web app that turns SPHEREx's public data into investigations anyone can run: give it a sky position, and it shows how that spot changed across SPHEREx's six-month visits, measured from the real calibrated images, with the evidence for and against every conclusion on screen.
