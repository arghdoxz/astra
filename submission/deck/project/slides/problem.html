THE PROBLEM

Moving-object searches drown in false positives

SATURATION

Bright stars break the detector

Barnard’s Star is saturated in 99% of its SPHEREx exposures. A naive centroid would “measure” a motion that isn’t there.

ARTIFACTS

Ghosts look like sources

SPHEREx flags halos, dichroic ghosts and persistence. Ignore the flags and every ghost becomes a candidate.

BLENDS

Neighbours steal the light

At WISE 0855, a G = 16.3 Gaia star 7.2″ away dominated the position, faking a 21σ disagreement with the catalogue.

STALE COORDINATES

Fast movers aren’t where you look

The WISE 0855 coordinates in the challenge are its ~2010 position, about 2 arcmin from where it is today.

ASTRA · Space Apps 202602
Planet X–style searches look for faint, cold, moving objects, and almost every apparent signal is something else. These four traps all showed up in our own validation runs on real SPHEREx data. The blend case is the one ASTRA caught in itself: before we added a Gaia blend check, the brown dwarf WISE 0855 appeared to disagree with its catalogue motion at 21 sigma. It was a brighter neighbour 7.2 arcseconds away.
