We ran ASTRA on the five targets from the challenge brief, where the right answer is already known. Only Eps Indi B gave a clean motion measurement. The others failed for real physical reasons: Barnard’s Star and R Dra are too bright for SPHEREx and saturate, and WISE 0855 sits next to a brighter star. A tool that reported strong detections here would be wrong; ASTRA reports why it can’t tell.
