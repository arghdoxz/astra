Eps Indi B is a pair of brown dwarfs about 12 light-years away, moving about 4.7 arcseconds a year. SPHEREx pixels are 6.15 arcseconds, so over 1.4 years it moves about one pixel. ASTRA still recovers the motion, consistent with the catalogue at 0.59 sigma, and fits a parallax freely that agrees within one sigma. The uncertainties are large and shown as such; this is the best motion test of the five targets.
